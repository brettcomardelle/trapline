TrapLine Support

TrapLine records the line you drive while setting crab traps, then helps you find it and run it again.

Common questions

  • The line keeps recording when the phone is locked. Just don’t swipe the app closed while recording. If it does get closed, open it again and the recording carries on.
  • No cell signal on the water? GPS and your lines still work. Map pictures for areas you haven’t viewed before may be blank, so look around your fishing area on Wi-Fi first.
  • Which phones and tablets: TrapLine needs GPS. On Apple, that’s any iPhone or a Wi-Fi + Cellular iPad (Wi-Fi-only iPads have no GPS, so the App Store doesn’t offer TrapLine for them). On Android, any phone or tablet with GPS.
  • Satellite map blurrier than usual? When the usual satellite pictures aren’t available, TrapLine shows backup pictures from the US government (US only, less sharp up close, no place names) and switches back by itself when it can.
  • Beep and cha-ching: the speaker and $ buttons on the map turn them off or on, and they stay that way until you change them. Check your media volume if you can’t hear them.
  • Back up a line: open the line in My Lines and choose “Share as GPX file”. Another phone can load it with My Lines → Import.
  • GPS accuracy is usually 10–30 ft. TrapLine is not for navigation. Always keep a proper lookout.
