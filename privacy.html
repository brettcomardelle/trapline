TrapLine Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version

TrapLine doesn’t collect your personal information. There’s no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your location

TrapLine uses your device’s location to show your boat on the map and to record your lines, including while the screen is locked during a recording.

Your positions, lines and trap marks are saved only on your device. TrapLine never sends them anywhere.

Maps

To draw the satellite map and the nautical charts, TrapLine downloads map pictures from Esri (satellite imagery), the US Geological Survey (backup satellite imagery, used when Esri’s isn’t available) and NOAA (charts). Like any website, those servers see your device’s internet address and which parts of the map are being loaded. They don’t receive your GPS position or your lines.

Sharing

A line leaves your device only when you choose “Share as GPX file”, and then only to wherever you send it.

Deleting your data

Delete a line from its page in My Lines. Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

Children

TrapLine isn’t directed at children and doesn’t collect information from anyone.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and in the app.
